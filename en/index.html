We will navigate the AI transformation as a society. No one is left behind.

Artificial intelligence is changing work, education and public administration. We don't know how fast it will go or who it will affect most. We study the effects on individuals and on society as a whole, and we look for what to do about them together. Anyone can take part.

The project is being founded in the Czech Republic. Our research covers the Czech Republic and the rest of the world.

How the idea began

In autumn 2026 I was at the TED AI conference in Vienna. There I realized that in a transformative time like this, it is not enough to follow the technology. We also have to look at the social impact of the changes.

I don't yet have ready answers to the questions this raises. That is why this place is being created: to look for them together.

Adam Hazdra, initiator of the project

What we look for answers to

We can't predict how fast the change will go, who will lose their job and who will benefit. Optimistic and catastrophic scenarios alike pretend to certainty. We don't want to. We ask:

  • What should I do myself?As an employee, entrepreneur, student or parent.
  • How should companies, legislation and the labour market respond?At the level of the system, not just the individual.
  • How do we make sure no one is left behind?No one, whether the change works out for them or not.

Main directions

The project stands on two directions, research and recommendations. Recommendations are built on research.

Research

We map the positive and negative impacts of the AI transformation on the lives of individuals and on society as a whole. In the Czech Republic and around the world.

We collect data, sources and people's experience. For every finding we say what is documented and what is still an estimate.

Recommendations

Research turns into recommendations for individuals, companies and public administration, including legislation and the labour market.

We formulate, discuss, refine and test the recommendations in practice. What does not hold up, we drop.

Why Stockdale

The name comes from Jim Collins's book Good to Great. Admiral Jim Stockdale survived eight years as a prisoner of war in Vietnam. The optimists who kept saying "we'll be out by Christmas" did not make it. Those who survived kept the faith that they would prevail in the end, and at the same time looked straight at the brutal facts.

We are in a similar situation with AI. We don't know how long the change will last or who it will affect. We want to go through it with open eyes and with the faith that we will manage it.

What we stand on

Open to everyone
Anyone can take part, from any field, however much or little they know about AI.
No political colour
We belong to no party or ideology. We judge by data and consequences.
No one is left behind
We assess every recommendation also from the point of view of those who adapt to change least easily.
Together
We support each other and aim at the same goal: navigating modern times.
Honesty
We say what we know and what we don't. We promise no certainty we don't have.
